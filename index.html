Nasima's Childcare / Local services / childcare

A clearer digital front door.

April 2025 - April 2026

Supporting a local childcare business with parent-friendly content, practical website updates and three Facebook campaigns that delivered 5.7K paid views.

The brief

Help a small Enfield childcare business present its services clearly to local parents. The content needed to feel reassuring, easy to understand and consistent with the information on its website.

My contribution

  • Created and managed Facebook content and designed posts in Canva.
  • Ran Meta Facebook Ads Manager campaigns aimed at local parents.
  • Updated website HTML content and imagery through GitHub, including funded-childcare information.

What this shows

Practical marketing support across channels: adapting messages for social, delivering targeted campaigns on modest budgets and keeping useful website information current.

*

Results in context

Three completed Facebook campaigns created by Meharin delivered 5.7K paid views and 3.4K combined reported reach on under GBP 40 total spend. Google Search Console also recorded 1.7K impressions and 55 clicks between 8 June 2025 and 30 April 2026. Meta reports reach per campaign, so the combined figure may include audience overlap.

  • Canva
  • Meta Ads Manager
  • HTML content
  • GitHub

Flora / Beauty / social creative

An idea with a natural flow.

Canva concept / portfolio refinement

A flowing-hair visual that turns a simple seasonal message into a recognisable beauty concept.

The creative challenge

Give a local beauty business a simple, distinctive seasonal social post. I wanted the image and the words to work together rather than compete for attention.

The idea

The hair becomes a waterfall: a visual metaphor for movement and flow. A restrained navy-and-white palette keeps the silhouette and brand name easy to recognise.

How I'd develop it

Pair the awareness post with a service-led Story and a clear booking link. I would test the message against a treatment-led version and compare booking-link clicks, not just likes.

*

From concept to next step

The original Canva idea is mine. This portfolio version refines the headline to 'Let your summer style flow'. The suggested rollout and measurement are proposals, not delivered campaign results.

  • Concept development
  • Social copy
  • Visual hierarchy

Boden / Fashion / seasonal creative

Let the product idea bloom.

Original Canva concept

A dress silhouette grows from a flower, connecting a summer message to the product category in one glance.

The creative challenge

Create a memorable seasonal fashion post without relying on a busy layout. The idea needed to communicate summer and clothing immediately.

The idea

The flower and dress share one silhouette, giving 'bloom' a visual meaning. Green, white space and a short headline make the concept feel fresh and easy to scan.

How I'd develop it

Extend the concept into a product-led carousel using approved collection photography, ending with a 'Shop summer dresses' call to action. I would compare product-page clicks and saves across the creative versions.

*

Clear project status

This is a self-initiated creative exercise, not work commissioned, approved or published by Boden. The proposed extension is a next-step idea, not a campaign result.

  • Canva
  • Visual storytelling
  • Seasonal messaging
  • Social copy

The Cheeky Panda / Consumer goods / brand communications

Making a big message feel approachable.

University brand PR exercise

A two-sided brochure concept exploring how a playful brand voice can make an environmental message more engaging for a Gen Z audience.

The brief

Create a university brand PR concept for The Cheeky Panda, balancing an environmental theme with the brand's playful personality and a young audience.

My contribution

Created the brochure concept in Canva, combining panda illustrations, bold green-and-orange sections, explanatory copy and calls to action across two sides.

What this shows

Thinking beyond a single social post: organising a longer message, adapting brand tone and building a visual sequence from an opening idea to a reader action.

*

An academic concept

This university exercise was not commissioned or approved by The Cheeky Panda. The draft copy is illustrative, not a statement of verified environmental performance; its claims would need checking before a real campaign.

  • Canva
  • Brand tone
  • Message hierarchy
  • Brochure design
